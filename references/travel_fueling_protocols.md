# Travel & Anti-Fragile Fueling Protocols

Adapted from "The Apex Nutrition Protocol" (Jack Krucial). Purpose: when a night
is already flagged as eat-out/travel — via calendar detection (`analyze_calendar.py`),
Q1 (`eat_out_nights`), or a Cameron-specific note in Q6 (`references/intake_schema.md`) —
use the matching scenario below instead of defaulting to a blank "eating out"
placeholder. These are fallback guidance for Claude and Austin to apply manually
when building or narrating a week's plan around a travel day; they do not change
how travel days are detected or logged.

Still built around the standing constraints: Austin 150g+ protein/day, no
avocado, no mango/peach/melon; Cameron 90–100g protein/day, avocado where it
fits, needs strong flavor + sauce.

---

## 1. Airport / Flight Lounge

- If nothing solid is available, it's fine to let one meal run light and make
  it up at the next real meal — don't force a bad option just to hit a number.
- Grab-first picks: a protein box / egg plate, grilled chicken or salmon from
  a sit-down terminal restaurant, a sushi counter (skip avocado rolls for
  Austin), Greek yogurt or a protein shake from a kiosk.
- Avoid: pretzels/pastries as a stand-in for a meal, sauced sandwiches with no
  visible protein, sugary "energy" drinks.

## 2. Airbnb With Kitchen Access

- Front-load breakfast and lunch at the rental so dinner out (or a planned
  restaurant night) has more slack. Use whatever's simplest to buy locally:
  eggs, Greek yogurt/skyr, a rotisserie or pan-seared protein, pre-washed
  greens.
- One grocery run on arrival covering 2–3 days of protein-forward
  breakfasts/lunches beats trying to eat out for every meal.
- Cameron: keep an avocado in the mix if the local store has one decent.

## 3. Hotel, Fridge-Only (No Kitchen)

- Lean on no-cook, high-protein staples: Greek yogurt or skyr, cottage
  cheese, pre-cooked deli chicken/turkey, canned tuna or salmon (pouches
  travel better than cans), hard-boiled eggs from a nearby grocery/deli.
- Bring or buy a protein powder as backup for a fast 25–30g hit when there's
  no real food option nearby.
- A nearby grocery store or deli case usually covers 1–2 days of
  breakfast/lunch even without a stove.

## 4. Restaurant Ordering Strategy

- Default to protein-forward formats that travel well across the existing
  cuisine rotation: steakhouse (grilled protein + veg side), sushi/sashimi
  (skip avocado rolls for Austin, fine for Cameron), Mexican/Tex-Mex
  burrito-bowl style (protein + rice + beans + veg, easy to scale Cameron's
  portion down), Japanese/Thai grilled or stir-fried protein dishes.
- Order protein first, then build the rest of the plate around it — ask for
  extra protein or a side salad if the base portion looks light against the
  150g/90–100g daily targets.
- Liquid calories (cocktails, sugary mixers) count against the 10% in the
  90/10 rule (see `user_profile.md` → Nutrition Philosophy) — fine
  occasionally, don't stack them with dessert on the same night without
  noticing.
