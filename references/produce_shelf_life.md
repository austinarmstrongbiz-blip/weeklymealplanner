# Produce Shelf Life: Two-Week Cycle Guide

One big shop covers 14 days. Plan so perishables are eaten first and sturdy produce
carries week 2. Every produce line on the grocery list gets a `use_window`.

| use_window | Badge in app | Means |
|---|---|---|
| `early` | Use days 1–5 | Spoils fast. Cook it, or prep it into something that keeps (pesto, pickles, sauce), in the first 5 days. |
| `mid` | Use days 5–9 | Holds about a week if stored right. |
| `late` | Keeps: days 8–14 | Holds 2+ weeks. This carries the second week. |

`buy: "midcycle"` marks the few things worth a quick top-up around day 7 or 8 (a $15 run, not a full shop).

---

## Early (days 1–5)
- Tender herbs: cilantro, basil, dill, mint, parsley. Store stems in water, loosely bagged. **Use up:** blitz the leftovers into chimichurri, green sauce or herb oil and freeze it in ice-cube trays.
- Leafy greens: spring mix, arugula, spinach, butter lettuce. **Use up:** wilt spinach and arugula into eggs, soup or pasta.
- Berries, ripe avocado (Cameron only), ripe tomatoes, mushrooms, bean sprouts, green onions
- Asparagus, green beans, zucchini, summer squash, corn on the cob
- Fresh fish and shellfish: cook within 1–2 days of buying, or buy frozen and thaw on schedule.

## Mid (days 5–9)
- Bell peppers, cucumbers, broccoli, cauliflower, Brussels sprouts, snap peas
- Kale, collards, romaine hearts (sturdier greens)
- Limes, lemons (2+ weeks in a zip bag in the fridge)
- Firm avocados: buy green and ripen on the counter so they're ready mid-cycle
- Ginger (or freeze it whole and grate from frozen)

## Late (days 8–14+)
- Cabbage (red/green/napa). The best week-2 vegetable: slaw, stir fry, roasted wedges, quick pickle.
- Carrots, celery, beets, radishes (greens off)
- Sweet potatoes, potatoes, onions, shallots, garlic (counter or pantry)
- Winter squash: butternut, kabocha, delicata, spaghetti (weeks on the counter)
- Apples, pears, citrus
- Fennel, celery root
- Frozen vegetables and frozen fruit
- ButcherBox and other frozen proteins (thaw on schedule; see the plan's `prep.phases[thaw]`)

---

## Make it last (Sunday moves)
- **Pickle what won't last:** red onion, cucumber, jalapeño, radish. Quick pickles keep 2 weeks and turn early produce into a late-week component.
- **Cook down what won't last:** soft tomatoes → sauce; wilting herbs → green sauce; spinach → frozen pucks.
- **Freeze sauces and marinades in flat bags** (vacuum sealed). A frozen marinade with the raw protein inside is a ready-made week-2 dinner: thaw it and cook it.
- **Don't pre-cut what doesn't hold:** avocado, apple, basil, and leafy greens get cut the day they're eaten. Peppers, onions, carrots, cabbage, celery and broccoli hold 4–5 days cut, in a container lined with a paper towel.
- **Cut produce for week 2 gets cut mid-cycle,** not on Sunday. That's what the prep's `midcycle` phase is for.
